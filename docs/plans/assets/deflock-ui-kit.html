Overview

A one-page leave-behind for city and county council members: what an ALPR network is, what your jurisdiction has already deployed, and the three questions to ask before the next contract renewal.

Print-ready, plain-language, and sourced — hand it across the dais and it stands on its own.

Talk Track

  • Open: "These cameras log every car that passes, not just suspects."
  • Cost: Annual Flock fees vs. clearance-rate evidence.
  • Ask: A public retention-and-access policy before renewal.

Rebuttals

  • "It only catches criminals." Every plate is stored — 99% belong to no case.
  • "We don't share the data." Audit logs show out-of-state and federal queries.
  • "There's oversight." SC has no statute governing ALPR retention or access.
